About Anurag Seksaria

Anurag started his entrepreneurial journey in January 2021 and currently running his own consulting firm focused on Capability Building and Outcome based consulting using Data Science, Six Sigma, Lean and related frameworks.

Over last three decades Anurag has experienced the journey of Statistics to Data Science and Quality to Business Excellence. He is Masters in Statistics with specialization in Quality Reliability and Operations Research from Indian Statical Institute.

Key Experience

Deutsche Bank

Created global capacity modeling & predictive modeling frameworks deployed to over 16 locations globally, helping businesses manage market-events-driven transaction processing volume spikes. Extended process excellence knowledge to automation and IT solution design and delivery.

Asea Brown Boveri (ABB)

Developed and delivered comprehensive training programs including "Quality in Design", "Quality in Manufacturing" and "Quality in Transaction Processing" across multiple ABB locations in India.

Aditya Birla Management Corporation

Built the Capability and Deployment framework and implemented it in Non Ferrous Metals companies in India and overseas units involved in Chemical, Textile and Fiber plants business.
